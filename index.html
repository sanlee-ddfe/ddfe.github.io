Welcome to DDFE

Understanding complex flows with data and physics.

Welcome to the Data-driven Fluid Engineering Lab (DDFE).

We develop AI-driven frameworks for fluid and physical systems, covering turbulence over rough surfaces, semiconductor process foundation models, quantum computing–enabled AI, and urban flow prediction and control, with an emphasis on data-driven and explainable modeling of complex multiscale phenomena.
