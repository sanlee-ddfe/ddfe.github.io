Welcome to DDFE!

Welcome to the Data-driven Fluid Engineering Lab (DDFE).

We develop AI-driven frameworks that accelerate the analysis and design of fluid engineering systems, covering turbulence over rough surfaces, semiconductor process foundation models, quantum-inspired (QI) AI, marine system design optimization, underground parking ventilation, and urban flow prediction and control, with an emphasis on data-driven and explainable modeling of complex multiscale phenomena.
